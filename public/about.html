What this site is and how to use it.

What is this?

A collection of pixel-art avatar decorations. Browse the gallery, click any decoration to see it on an avatar, and share your own pixel art in the Showcase.

How do I use it?

  1. Open the Gallery and pick a set, or search by name.
  2. Click a decoration to preview it larger.
  3. Log in with Yapin’ to see your profile picture in the previews.

Sharing your work

Log in with Yapin’, open the Showcase tab and press Submit. Only upload art you made yourself. New submissions may wait for approval before they appear.

Questions

Does the site see my password?

No. Yapin’ handles the login and only shares your name, username and profile picture.

How do I remove my access?

In Yapin’ go to Settings → Authorized Apps and remove this site.

Can I use the decorations?

They are here to look at and try on. Please don’t repost them as your own. If you want to use one somewhere, ask in the support server first.

How do I share one decoration?

Open it and press Copy link. The link shows the decoration’s name and picture when you paste it into chat.

How do I report something?

On the Showcase tab, press Report under any work that breaks the rules. A few reports hide it until the site owner checks.

Want to partner with us?

Check the Partners page, or ask in the support server.
